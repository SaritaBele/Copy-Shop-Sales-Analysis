# Copy-Shop-Sales-Analysis
The dataset taken here is the coffee shop sales dataset. 

**Business Problem:** The main objective of this project is to analyze retail sales data to gain actionable insights that will enhance the performance of the coffee shop.

**Recomended Analysis:** Location wise sales,Hours based sale,Monthly and week days sales,
KPI's Total Revenue, Total footfall,Avg Sales per person, Avg order per person,Avg month sales.

**Report Analysis:**
**(A) Sales by Hours:**
-Peak time : Is from 8am to 10pm
-Slowest/Steady Hours: Is starting by sudden drop in sales sharply after 10am to 11am and then remains steady/slow from 11am to 6pm.
(**Insights:** 1. For Peak time train the staff, that while serving orders ask cutomer for other products with coffee like bakery products,which will impact on customers mindset.
2. For Peak time offer Morning Combos.
3. For Slow afternoons also can also offer Afternoon Combos to increase the foot fall).

**(B) Sales by Week Days:**
- Busy Day: Friday , as compare to monday and thursday with a small hike.
- Slowest Days: Saturdays and Sundays, evwn if it is a weekend the footfall and sales are less.
- (**Insights:** To boost weekends can launch brunch offers, special weekend combos, can be a partner with food delivery so can cover up local area by delivery at there locations).

**(C) Top performer Product Catrgory:**
- Coffee with overall 39% involvemnt
- Follwed by Tea 28%
**(D) Lowest Performer Product:**
-Branded(2%)
-Flavours(1%)
-Loose tea(1&)
-Packaged Chocolate.
(**Insights:** 1.The Top perfomer product Coffee can increse more with by serving different coffee beans, filter coffee.
2. Followed by Tea ,Sales can be increased by free samples of flavour teas, Loose tea.
3. The Branded and Packaged Chocolate can be combine with the Top performer product coffee).

**(E) Top Location in Foot fall:** Hell'S kitchen with high foot fall.
**(F) Top Location Sales:** Hell's Kitchen has highest sales as $2,36,511.17, but if we compare with Astoria and Lower Manhattan , the top sales does not show that much hike in sales.
(**Insights:** 1. Even if the Footfall is high at Hells kitchen the sales does not show the that much hike as compare to other locations, here also the we need to apply some Sales Strategy to increase the overall location sales.)

**(G)** As per the KPI's the Avg sales person is $4.69 per visit and Avg order per person is 1.44 items.
(**Insights:** Avg sales person is $4.69 per visit need to be increase as per above suggested insights and Avg order per person is 1.44 items this can increased with the help of Combo offers).
